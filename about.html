Since 2023

Started with a craving. Built on the crunch.

Kono Crisp began with a simple idea: fries deserve to be the main event, not a side. So we built a menu around them: fried fresh to order, shaken in bold dry seasonings and served with dips you’ll want on everything.

We keep things focused. One kitchen on Model Town Link Road, Lahore, where every batch gets the same care, from our Crave Classic to the Habanero Firestorm.
